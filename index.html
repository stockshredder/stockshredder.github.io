Fully supported: MP4, MOV, MXF, MKV, AVI, M4V, MTS, M2TS, MPG, MPEG, WebM, 3GP, WMV. Covers standard DJI, Sony, and ARRI camera files.

Blackmagic RAW (.braw) is supported natively. RED R3D is accepted with REDCINE-X Pro installed. CinemaDNG sequences (DJI, Blackmagic) are supported on macOS. Legacy ARRIRAW (.ari/.arx) works with ARRI’s ART CMD tool installed.

Not yet supported: modern ALEXA 35-style ARRIRAW/ARRICORE recorded straight to .mxf, and Canon Cinema RAW Light. Phantom Cine isn't read directly, but Windows users can convert it first with Phantom's own free PCC software. Nikon N-RAW also isn't supported.
